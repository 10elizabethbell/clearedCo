# Design System: ClearedCo

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Swept Driveway"**

The page reads like a job just finished: clean, ordered, nothing left lying around. It is Ellie's house calm band style. Full-bleed bands alternate between a cool off-white paper and two indigos taken from the logo, so every new section is obvious at a glance on a phone. One warm gold carries every action. The only photograph in the hero is the client's own washed driveway, held under an indigo veil so white type sits on it without effort.

Density is low and generous: one idea per band, a 20px phone gutter, 64px of band padding on phones and 96px from tablet width. Shapes are soft and borderless (24px cards, full pills, tinted fields), depth comes from band colour and translucent white glass, not from shadows. Motion is small and earned: the hero headline bounces in letter by letter once, things below the first screen rise 12px as they arrive, each before/after slider sweeps once to show it moves, and a single gold contact circle floats in after the hero.

The earlier "fogged dusk window" world (mist, painted frames, canvas fog, water seams) is gone and is not part of this system.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Alternating full-bleed bands: paper, navy, paper, deep indigo with a faint 135deg line texture.
- One warm accent, gold, used as a fill; gold text only on dark bands, a darker gold-ink on light.
- Cal Sans for every heading and every tappable label; the system stack for running text at 16/1.7.
- Soft borderless shapes on a fixed radius ladder (24 / 20 / 18 / 16 / 10 / 8 / pill).
- Flat surfaces; only the slider knob and the floating circle cast a shadow.
- Real photography only: the client's driveway and three real before/after pairs.

## Colors

Cool paper and the logo's indigos, with one warm gold doing all the pointing.

### Primary
- **Porch-Light Gold** (`gold`): the one accent. Fills every primary button, the service-row arrow on hover, the before/after divider and knob, the step numbers, the floating contact circle, text selection, and the accent phrase in headings on dark bands. Text on it is always ink.
- **Warm Gold Hover** (`gold-hover`): the primary button's hover fill only.
- **Gold Ink** (`gold-ink`): gold when it has to be text on a light ground (the accent phrase in a paper-band heading). Never a fill.

### Secondary
- **Logo Indigo** (`navy`): the services band, the closing band, and the Facebook row on paper.
- **Night Indigo** (`deep`): the "How it works" band and the footer, both carrying the line texture; also the hero's base under the photo. Its translucent forms are the hero veils (`hero-veil-light` .62, `hero-veil-mid` .78, `hero-veil` .80, `hero-veil-dense` .90) and the photo tag backing (`tag-veil` .6).
- **Logo Violet** (`violet`): the message icon beside "Message us" in the header. Also the light end of the favicon gradient (ink to violet).
- **Focus Violet** (`focus`): every hover and focus outline on light grounds: form fields, chips, form buttons, the header link hover, the slider knob focus ring. Kept distinct from gold so a focused control never looks like a filled one.

### Neutral
- **Cool Paper** (`paper`): page ground, header, paper bands, the form's done panel. Also `on-dark`, the heading colour on indigo bands.
- **White** (`card`): the quote form card, the done panel's textarea, photo tag text, the hero H1, the selected chip's text.
- **Field Lilac** (`field`): text inputs and chips on the white card, and the before/after placeholder behind loading photos.
- **Ink** (`ink`): headings on light, text on gold, the selected chip fill, the ghost button on light.
- **Body Indigo** (`body`): running text on light.
- **Soft Indigo** (`soft`): ledes, hints, placeholder text, the brand sub-line, small print.
- **Mist Lilac** (`on-dark-soft`): running text and ledes on the indigo bands, the footer and the hero promise.
- **Error Red** (`error`): inline error sentences and the error border on a bad field.
- **Glass whites** on indigo: `glass-row` (.06) behind service rows; `glass-arrow` (.1) behind row arrows and the ghost button hover; `glass-raised` (.12) behind the Facebook row's arrow and the eyebrow on dark; `line-texture` (.035) for the diagonal lines.
- **Ink wash** (`ink-wash`, .06): the eyebrow backing on paper.
- **Ghost border** (`ghost-border`, .7 paper): the hero ghost button's 2px border; it goes solid white on hover.

### Named Rules
**The Gold Is A Fill Rule.** Gold is a fill with ink on it. As text it appears only on indigo (the heading accent phrase, the closing phone link hover, footer link hover); on paper the same phrase uses gold-ink.

**The Two Indigos Rule.** A dark band is either logo indigo (plain) or night indigo (with the line texture). No third dark, and never black.

**The Faint Lines Rule.** The deep-band texture is `repeating-linear-gradient(135deg, transparent 0 22px, line-texture 22px 23px)` and stays at 3.5% white so it never reads as lines through text.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Cal Sans (Google Fonts, loaded non-blocking with `display=swap`), falling back to Avenir Next, Segoe UI, system-ui.
**Body Font:** the system stack: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, Segoe UI, Helvetica Neue, Arial.

**Character:** Cal Sans is round, friendly and a little bold at weight 400; it handles every heading and every thing you tap (buttons, rows, the phone link, form labels), so actions read as headings rather than as fine print. The system body stays quiet and fast.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (400, `display` clamp 42px to 80px, 1.02): the hero H1 only, white, at most 12ch wide, with the place name in gold.
- **Headline** (400, `headline` clamp 30px to 48px, 1.08, -0.005em): every band's H2, balanced, with the last phrase in the accent where it says something.
- **Contact display** (400, `contact-display` clamp 30px to 52px, 1): the closing band's big "Message us" link.
- **Title** (400, 21px, 1.2): step titles (22px from 1024px), the done panel heading, and the wordmark (21px, 24px from 760px).
- **Facebook row title** (400, `title-fb` clamp 19px to 22px, 1.2).
- **Row title** (400, 20px, 1.2): service rows and the step numbers.
- **Button** (400, 18px, 1): every pill button.
- **Label** (400, 17px, 1.2): form labels and legends, and the header "Message us" link (20px from 760px).
- **Promise** (400, `promise` clamp 17px to 20px, 1.5): the hero promise line, 30ch on phones, unbounded from 760px.
- **Lede** (400, `lede` clamp 17px to 19px, 1.7, 38ch): section introductions. The closing band's line uses `lede-close` (clamp 18px to 20px).
- **Body** (400, 16px, 1.7): running text, chips, footer. Inputs are 16px / 1.4 so iOS never zooms.
- **Small** (400, 15px): the "Drag across a photo" hint, inline errors, the line under the send button.
- **Hint** (400, 14px, 1): "(optional)" beside a field label.
- **Tag** (500, 13px, 1, 0.04em): the Before / After tags on photos.
- **Brand sub-line** (600, 11px, 1, 0.08em, uppercase): "Canfield, Ohio" under the wordmark, nowhere else.

### Named Rules
**The Tappable Is Display Rule.** Anything you can press is set in Cal Sans; running text never is.

**The One Weight Rule.** Cal Sans is only ever used at 400. Hierarchy comes from size, not weight.

## Layout

A single centred column, max 1160px, with a 20px gutter on phones and 40px from 760px. Bands run full-bleed; content inside them is left-aligned, with section heads capped at 640px and ledes at 38ch.

- **Band rhythm:** 64px top and bottom on phones, 96px from 760px. The hero copy sits 56px / 72px from its edges on phones, 96px / 112px from 760px. The footer has 48px on top and 96px plus the safe area below so the floating circle never covers its text.
- **Stack gaps:** 8 (field label to input), 10 (chips, service rows), 12 (hero buttons, done panel), 14 (footer), 16 (head stack, pairs, steps columns), 20 (hero copy, form fields), 24 (steps on phones), 28 (form head to card), 32 (heading to rows or pairs), 36 (heading to steps).
- **Breakpoints:** 760px (tablet and up: wider gutter and bands, hero buttons side by side at min 220px, two-column service rows with a 16px gap, the before/after grid as 4fr / 3fr with the tall pair spanning two rows and capped at 880px, done-panel buttons side by side); 1024px (the quote head becomes sticky at 40px beside the form in a 1fr / 1.2fr grid with a 64px gap; the closing band puts the big link beside the name with a 48px gap; the three steps sit in a row with a 40px gap). Below 380px chips tighten to 14px side padding.
- **Hover only where it exists:** every hover style sits inside `(hover: hover)` so taps never leave a stuck state.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat by default. Depth comes from the band colour change and from translucent white glass on indigo, not from shadows. The hero gets its depth from the photo under the indigo veil: a vertical veil (.80 to .78 to .90) on phones over a photo softened with a 3px blur, and a horizontal veil (.90 to .80 to .62) on wider screens over the sharp photo, darkest behind the text.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Knob lift** (`box-shadow: 0 6px 16px -6px rgba(0,0,0,.5)`): the gold before/after knob only, so it reads as something to grab.
- **Floating circle** (`box-shadow: 0 10px 28px rgba(20,16,48,.32)`): the floating contact circle only, so it separates from whatever band is under it.

### Named Rules
**The Two Shadows Rule.** Only things that float over content (the slider knob and the contact circle) cast a shadow. Cards, rows, buttons and the form stay flat.

## Shapes

Soft and borderless. Corners step down with the size of the thing: 24px for the form card and the before/after photos, 20px for the Facebook row, 18px for service rows, 16px for the done panel, 10px for inputs, 8px for photo tags, a full pill for buttons, chips and the eyebrow, and full circles for arrows, step numbers, the knob, the logo and the floating circle. The keyboard focus ring follows a 6px radius (round on circles). Nothing has a visible border at rest; a 2px transparent border is reserved on buttons, inputs and chips so hover and focus can colour it without shifting layout.

## Components

### Buttons
Round, warm and big enough for a thumb.
- **Shape:** full pill, 56px tall, 26px side padding, 2px border, 20px icon with a 10px gap.
- **Primary:** gold fill and border, ink text. Hover goes to gold-hover. Inside the form it also gets a 2px focus-violet outline at 2px offset on hover and focus.
- **Ghost (on dark):** transparent, on-dark text, ghost-border edge; hover makes the border solid white over glass-arrow. **Ghost (on light, done panel):** ink text and ink border; hover fills white.
- **Press:** scales to .98 over .12s. Colour changes ease over .2s.
- In the form the submit button runs full width.

### Chips
- **Style:** field-lilac pill, 48px tall, 18px side padding, ink 16px text, 10px apart, wrapping.
- **State:** hover and keyboard focus colour the border focus-violet; selected fills ink with white text; selected plus focus adds a 3px focus-violet outline at 2px offset so it stays visible on the dark fill. Tapping a service row pre-selects its chip.

### Cards / Containers
- **Form card:** white, 24px corners, padding clamp(20px, 5vw, 32px), 20px between fields, max 600px.
- **Done panel:** paper, 16px corners, 18px padding, 12px gaps, a white 140px textarea, and a primary plus on-light ghost button pair.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** field-lilac fill, 2px transparent border, 10px corners, 13px / 14px padding, min 52px tall (textarea 110px, resizable), ink text, soft placeholder, navy caret.
- **Hover / Focus:** the border turns focus-violet (.15s); no outline.
- **Error:** a plain-sentence message in error red at 15px under the field, and an error-red border that wins over focus.

### Navigation
A slim paper header, 64px tall (76px from 760px): the round 40px logo (46px from 760px) and the ClearedCo wordmark with its sub-line on the left, "Message us" with a violet icon on the right; its hover turns focus-violet. No sticky bar and no menu. The footer is night indigo with the line texture and holds the legal name, area and the Facebook link (44px tap height, hover gold).

### Service rows
The services as plain rows on logo indigo. Each row is the link: glass-row fill, 18px corners, min 64px, row-title type, with a 44px glass arrow circle on the right. On hover only the arrow changes (to gold with ink); the row itself keeps its fill and gets no shadow. Keyboard focus moves the gold fill and a 3px gold outline to the arrow. One column on phones, two from 760px.

### Before / after slider
Three real pairs. Each photo sits at its own aspect ratio in a 24px-corner frame on field lilac. The "before" image is clipped to the divider position. The divider is a plain 3px gold line with a 44px gold knob carrying a chevron pair and the knob lift shadow. Before / After tags sit 14px from the top corners. Drag sideways or tap anywhere to move it; arrow keys work through a hidden range input whose focus puts a 3px focus-violet ring on the knob. The first time each slider is 60% in view it sweeps once (50 to 74 to 30 to 50 percent, 700ms per leg, ease in-out, after a 450ms wait for the fade-up); any touch cancels it.

### Facebook row
A navy row on paper under the photos: 20px corners, min 76px, max 520px, a 30px Facebook mark beside the label and a glass-raised arrow circle. Same rule as the service rows: only the arrow changes on hover.

### Steps
Three numbered steps on night indigo: a 48px gold circle with the number in ink, a 21px title in on-dark and a mist line under it. Stacked on phones, a row of three from 1024px.

### Floating contact circle
One 60px gold circle with a 28px message icon, 16px from the bottom right plus safe area, with the floating circle shadow. It slides up from 120px below (.35s, cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1)) once the hero is passed, and hides wherever it would sit on another control (service rows, the Facebook row, the form, the closing band). It is never a bottom bar.

### Motion
- **Hero H1 bounce-in:** each letter slides in from 50px right with a slight overshoot, 1s cubic-bezier(.34,1.56,.64,1), 20ms apart; the plain heading returns when the last letter lands. Hero H1 only.
- **Rise:** content that starts below the first screen fades up 12px over .4s ease-out as it arrives. Everything is visible at rest without JavaScript.
- **Reduced motion:** no letter animation, no rise, no slider sweep, no transitions on buttons, arrows or the circle, and smooth scrolling off.

### Owner-approved exception: eyebrow pill
Three short labels ship above the hero H1, "Recent work" and "Free quote": a 14px / 500 pill with 9px / 14px padding, an ink-wash backing on paper or glass-raised on dark, and a 7px gold dot. Ellie made this part of her house style on 2026-10-19 against Impeccable's guidance. It is recorded so the build is described accurately, not as a pattern to spread: no new eyebrows, never on every heading, never a button look (no arrow, hover, shadow or full gold fill), and marked up as text, never as a heading.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** alternate full-bleed bands (paper, navy, paper, deep with the 3.5% line texture) so each new section is obvious.
- **Do** put ink text on every gold fill, and switch accent text on paper to gold-ink.
- **Do** keep hover and focus outlines on light grounds focus-violet, and switch them to gold on indigo bands, the hero and the footer.
- **Do** give every control a 44px or larger tap target, and inputs 16px or larger type.
- **Do** keep the before/after divider a plain 3px gold line with the round gold knob.
- **Do** wrap every hover style in `(hover: hover)` and honour reduced motion.

### Don't:
- **Don't** add a sticky header or a bottom contact bar; the one floating circle is the only fixed element.
- **Don't** show an email address anywhere except the footer.
- **Don't** change a service row or the Facebook row on hover beyond its arrow: no fill change, no lift, no shadow.
- **Don't** decorate the slider divider (no glow, gradient, handles or labels on the line).
- **Don't** use gold as text on paper or white, or white text on gold.
- **Don't** add shadows to cards, rows, buttons or the form.
- **Don't** add letter animation to any heading other than the hero H1, or bring back the fog, mist, painted frames or water seams.
